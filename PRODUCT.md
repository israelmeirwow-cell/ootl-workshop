# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
People aged 16+ (men and women) who arrive mostly from Instagram and TikTok on a phone. Three situations: tried courses or a first business and nothing moved; have a small business that is stuck; want to start and don't know in what. They decide in one visit whether to pay for a seat.

## Product Purpose
A landing page that sells seats to a one-day, 4-hour hands-on workshop in Tel Aviv by יציאה מהלופ (a personal business mentoring program: from confusion to the first customer in 60 days). Success = a paid seat. The form saves the lead to a Google Sheet, then the visitor pays (Bit or card), then lands on a thank-you screen with the WhatsApp group link.

## Positioning
A workshop of doing, not inspiration: every block is focused knowledge, then doing it on your own idea or business in the room, personal feedback from mentors walking between tables, and a second try. Built from the tools of the paid mentoring program (3,500 ש״ח). 25 seats, priced up as they sell.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Copy comes from the client's brief (Claude Docs, 2026-10-12) and is final. Do not add copy, emoji or exclamation marks beyond it. Neutral Hebrew addressed to everyone (לך, שלך, plural).
- Tiered seats: 12 at 49/89 (pair), 8 at 69/119, 5 at 97/159. 97 shown struck while not the full price. A seat counts only after payment; the count is updated by hand (or from the sheet).
- Sold out: CTA becomes "הסדנה מלאה — הצטרפות לרשימת המתנה" and the form stays open for the waitlist.
- Tracking: Meta + TikTok pixels, Lead on form submit, Purchase on the thank-you screen, UTM kept with each lead.
- Payment: Bit is required by the user (2026-10-13). Bit works through a payment provider link (Grow, Morning, PayMe…) or a manual transfer to a Bit number. Exact links still pending.
- Open (from the brief): date and hours, address, partner's name/text/photo, Raz's photo, approved texts and photos of Alon and Miriam, Miriam's surname, payment link, WhatsApp group link.

## Brand Commitments
Part of the יציאה מהלופ identity already built in `out_of_the_loop_vallora/`: cream, ink and the red loop accent (#e0442f), Heebo, Frank Ruhl Libre for the brand wordmark, and the loop line that tangles and leaves straight to a red dot. The brief asks for a light background, one strong accent used only for buttons and important numbers, big bold headings, and a clean workshop/accelerator feel, not a party.

## Evidence on Hand
No photos yet. Only real photos of Raz, the partner and the lecturers may be used; no stock and no generated people. No testimonials or attendance numbers exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles
- Doing over inspiration: show the mechanism (block → doing → feedback → second try), not adjectives.
- Honest scarcity: real seat numbers only.
- Phone first: 375px, sticky registration bar, 17px+ body text, fast load.
